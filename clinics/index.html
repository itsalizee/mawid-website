What Mawid is — and isn't

Front-desk booking. Not an EHR.

Mawid handles patient appointments, confirmations, and reminders. It stores names, phone numbers, visit history, and any notes your team chooses to add — same level of data as a paper appointment book.

Mawid does not store medical records, lab results, prescriptions, diagnoses, or insurance claims. We're a booking platform, not regulated medical software. Your existing EHR (or your team) handles that, and we integrate with nothing — we just keep the front desk smooth.

03 How it works

Three steps. No medical complexity. No setup fees.

Your patients' experience is as simple as scheduling a coffee. Your team's experience is one screen showing today's appointments by hour.

01 · PATIENT

Picks a time.

You share your booking link from your Instagram bio or DM. Patient picks consultation time + adds an optional note ("follow-up for last week's filler"), enters their name and number. Thirty seconds.

02 · PATIENT

Gets confirmed.

WhatsApp confirmation arrives instantly with the date in Hijri and Gregorian. 24h before the appointment, a reminder. 2h before, a nudge with your clinic's location pin.

03 · RECEPTION

Sees who's coming.

Your appointments dashboard shows today by hour: patient name, phone, optional note. Tap to mark them arrived, completed, or no-show. No medical chart, no insurance form — just the front desk.

06 FAQ

Common questions from Gulf clinics.

Is Mawid HIPAA / Saudi PDPL compliant?

Mawid does not handle Protected Health Information (PHI) or anything that falls under HIPAA or Saudi PDPL medical data scope. We store patient names, phone numbers, appointment times, and optional free-text notes — the same data category as a paper appointment book. Your existing EHR keeps the medical record. If you need an EHR + billing suite, talk to platforms like Cura, Medscope, or Yashfeen instead — that's not what Mawid does.

Do you integrate with NPHIES / Sehhaty / our EHR?

Not in V1. Mawid is a standalone front-desk booking platform. Your team uses Mawid for appointments + reminders, and continues using your existing EHR (or Sehhaty for public-sector connectivity) for medical workflows. Direct integrations are on the roadmap once we have enough customer demand for specific integrations.

Can patients reschedule via WhatsApp?

Yes. Every confirmation includes a tap-to-reschedule link. The change reaches your dashboard in real time so the front desk always sees the truth.

Do we need a website?

No. Your Mawid booking link works on its own. Paste it into your Instagram bio, send it via WhatsApp, or QR-code it on a poster at reception. It's a full booking page, not a widget you have to embed.

What about insurance verification?

Out of scope for V1. Mawid asks the patient an optional "insurance provider" free-text field at booking so your team has it before the visit. We don't run real-time eligibility checks — your existing billing system or third-party tool handles that.

How many doctors can use one account?

The plan scales with capacity, not headcount. You tell us how many concurrent appointments your clinic can take (e.g. 3 providers on shift = 3 concurrent slots). One Mawid account can run a multi-provider clinic.

Which clinic types does Mawid work for?

Best fit: dermatology, aesthetic, cosmetic, dental, and GP clinics where appointments are time-bookable and the front-desk workflow is the bottleneck. Less suited: complex specialty clinics with insurance pre-authorisation workflows, or hospitals with department-level scheduling.

How long does it take to set up?

Under five minutes. Clinic name, country, how many concurrent appointments, opening hours. We give you a booking link. That's the install.
