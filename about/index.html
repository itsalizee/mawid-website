01 About Mawid

Built for the Gulf, by people who understand it.

Every booking platform serving the GCC was built somewhere else, then translated. Some are global tools with an Arabic toggle. Others tried to localize after the fact. None were engineered ground-up for the way Saudi, Emirati, Qatari, and Kuwaiti businesses actually run. Mawid is.

02 The story

Why Mawid exists.

Anyone who has tried to book a salon, clinic, or fitness studio in Riyadh, Dubai, Doha, or Kuwait City knows the drill. You DM them on Instagram. They ask for your phone number. You wait for a WhatsApp confirmation that may or may not come. You show up and hope your time is still booked.

On the other side of that conversation, the salon owner is running fourteen WhatsApp threads, one calendar in Google Sheets, another in their head, and a stack of paper notes for color formulas and customer preferences. No-shows hit twenty to thirty percent. Reactivation campaigns are nonexistent. Every booking is a manual reconstruction.

The global platforms can't fix this — they don't speak Arabic dialects, don't render Hijri dates, don't speak the Gulf customer's language, and don't understand that Friday morning is for prayer. The local platforms can't fix it either — they're underbuilt, with weak product and no investment.

Mawid is the platform we wished existed. Native to four GCC markets from day one. Built around Instagram and WhatsApp, because that's where bookings actually start. Arabic-first, with Hijri and Gregorian dates side by side. A searchable customer database that remembers every visit, every preference, every name.

03 What we believe

Four principles we don't compromise on.

These aren't aspirational poster slogans. Every product decision either lines up with these or gets cut.

01

Native, not translated.

We don't ship a global product with Arabic strings glued on. Every service, staff profile and booking page is bilingual at the data level, and the booking page shows Hijri dates because that's how much of the Gulf plans its week.

02

WhatsApp before everything.

Gulf customers live on WhatsApp and Instagram. They won't download your app or remember another password. So every customer-facing flow we build starts with one question: does this work from a chat? If not, we redesign it.

03

Honest pricing, no hidden fees.

One flat monthly subscription, published on the pricing page. No commission on bookings, no surcharges, no extra fees for support. You see exactly what you pay.

04

Ship, then improve, with real customers.

We'd rather put Mawid in front of real salons, clinics and restaurants, watch what breaks and fix it fast, than polish slides. Every customer gets a direct line to the people building it.

04 The company

Diyar Holdings.

Mawid is operated by Diyar Holdings LLC, a Wyoming-registered company purpose-built to scale technology products into Gulf markets. We chose Wyoming for the legal infrastructure — clean US incorporation, banking, and payment processing — while operating natively in the GCC where our customers are.
