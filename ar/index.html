04 Customer experience

How they book.

Your customers go from Instagram link to confirmed appointment in under sixty seconds. They pay your business directly through Mada, Apple Pay, KNET, or Tabby — using your existing merchant account. Mawid never touches the money. We bill a transparent platform fee monthly that scales down as you scale up.

  • 01

    They find you on Instagram.

    Your branded booking link sits in your bio, in your stories, and in your DMs. One tap and they're on your page — no download, no signup.

  • 02

    They book in their language.

    Your public booking page renders in Arabic or English based on their phone. Services, prices, calendar — all of it bilingual at the row level.

  • 03

    They pay you directly.

    Mada, Apple Pay, KNET, Tabby — whichever they already use. The payment lands in your account, not ours. No-shows drop sixty percent the first month.

  • 04

    WhatsApp confirms. Forever.

    Confirmation, reminders, reschedules, follow-ups — all on the channel they use every day. You see every booking the moment it lands.
