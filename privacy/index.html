1. Who we are

Mawid is operated by Diyar Holdings LLC, a limited liability company registered in the State of Wyoming, United States of America (registered office: 30 N Gould Street, Suite R, Sheridan, WY 82801). For the purposes of this policy, "Mawid", "we", "us", and "our" refer to Diyar Holdings LLC.

This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share personal information when you use our website at mawid.ai, our software-as-a-service booking platform, our public booking pages, and any related services (collectively, the "Services").

We comply with the principles of the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), the United Arab Emirates Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data, the Qatar Personal Data Protection Law, the Kuwait Data Privacy Protection Regulation, and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where applicable.

2. Information we collect

We collect different types of information depending on whether you are (a) a business customer using Mawid to manage bookings, (b) a staff member of a business customer, or (c) an end-customer booking an appointment through a Mawid-powered booking page.

From business customers and their staff

  • Account data: business name, owner name, contact email, phone number, business address, country of operation, vertical (salon, clinic, etc.), and information needed to verify your business.
  • Authentication data: hashed passwords, one-time codes sent to your phone for sign-in, and session tokens.
  • Billing data: billing address and payment instrument tokens (we do not store full card numbers — these are stored by our payment processor).
  • Service usage data: services you offer, prices, staff schedules, business hours, and the configuration choices you make.
  • Support and communications: the content of any emails, WhatsApp messages, or support tickets you send us.

From end-customers (people booking appointments)

  • Booking data: name, phone number, email (if provided), the service booked, date and time, the staff member (if selected), and the business at which the booking is made.
  • Payment data: deposit or full prepayment amounts, payment method type (Mada, Apple Pay, KNET, Tabby, Tamara, etc.), and a reference token from the payment processor. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Communications: messages you exchange with the business via WhatsApp, SMS, or email, where these flow through our platform.
  • Service-specific data: any preferences you provide (such as color formulas, allergies, fitness goals, treatment notes) when booking with a particular business.

Automatic data collection

  • IP address, device type, browser type, operating system, language preference, and approximate location (city-level) derived from IP.
  • Pages visited, links clicked, time spent, and referring website.
  • Crash reports and performance data.

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Services — including processing bookings, sending confirmations and reminders, and routing payments.
  • Authenticate users, prevent fraud, and protect the security of accounts.
  • Send transactional communications (booking confirmations, appointment reminders, password resets, billing notices).
  • Respond to support requests and communicate with you about the Services.
  • Improve the Services through aggregate analysis of how features are used. We do not sell or share individual user data for this purpose.
  • Comply with legal obligations, enforce our terms, and protect our rights.

For business customers, we use the data you upload (your customer database, services, schedules) only to operate the Services on your behalf. We do not sell your customer data, do not market to your customers without your explicit instruction, and do not share your data with other Mawid customers.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We share information only as follows:

  • With service providers (sub-processors) who help us operate Mawid — including hosting (Vercel, Neon), authentication (Clerk), payments (Stripe for our billing, Tap Payments for customer-side flows), messaging (Unifonic for WhatsApp/SMS, Resend for email), analytics (Vercel Analytics, Posthog), and AI processing (Anthropic). All sub-processors are bound by data processing agreements.
  • With your business customer — if you book an appointment through a Mawid-powered page, the booking information is shared with the business that provided the page, as that business needs the data to deliver the service to you.
  • With law enforcement or regulators when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the safety of users or the public.
  • In connection with a business transfer — if Mawid or Diyar Holdings is acquired, merges with another company, or sells substantially all of its assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. You will be notified before this happens.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with advertisers for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Data security

We protect personal information using technical and organizational measures appropriate to the risks involved. These include:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) for all connections to our Services.
  • Encryption at rest for all data stored in our primary database.
  • Multi-factor authentication for staff access to production systems.
  • Role-based access controls so only authorized personnel can access customer data.
  • Regular security audits, dependency scanning, and penetration testing.
  • Incident response procedures and breach notification commitments aligned with applicable law.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is one hundred percent secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. International transfers

Mawid is operated from the United States, with primary infrastructure in AWS Frankfurt (European Union). When you use the Services, your personal information may be transferred to, processed in, or accessed from countries other than the country in which you reside.

For customers in the GCC, this means your data may be processed in the EU or US. We rely on the appropriate legal mechanisms for these transfers — including Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers from the EU, and the data subject's explicit consent where required under KSA PDPL or UAE PDP Law for cross-border transfers.

If you are a business customer with regulatory requirements that data remain within a specific country, contact us — we offer regional data residency options on Enterprise plans.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: request that we delete your information, subject to our legal obligations.
  • Portability: receive your information in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: object to specific uses of your information.
  • Withdrawal of consent: withdraw consent where we rely on it as the legal basis for processing.
  • Complaint: file a complaint with your local data protection authority (the SDAIA in Saudi Arabia, the UAE Data Office, the Qatar National Cyber Security Agency, or the Kuwaiti CITRA).

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@mawid.ai. We will respond within thirty days.

8. Children's data

The Services are not directed at children under the age of sixteen. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact privacy@mawid.ai and we will delete it.

Where the Services are used by businesses that serve minors (such as pediatric clinics or family salons), the business is responsible for obtaining the appropriate parental consent before submitting any minor's data to the Services.

9. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Services, remember your preferences, authenticate sessions, and understand how the Services are used. Specifically:

  • Essential cookies: required for the Services to function (authentication, session management, language preference). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: we use first-party analytics to understand aggregate usage. These can be disabled in your browser without affecting the Services.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies on our marketing website.

10. Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Specifically:

  • Account data: retained while your account is active. Deleted within ninety days of account closure unless legal retention requirements apply.
  • Booking data: retained for seven years after the appointment date for tax and accounting purposes (consistent with GCC commercial record requirements).
  • Communications: retained for three years from the date of last interaction.
  • Backups: retained for thirty days, after which they are permanently destroyed.

11. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify business customers by email and update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. We will not make changes that reduce your rights under this policy without your explicit consent.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy, your information, or how to exercise your rights, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@mawid.ai
  • Mail: Diyar Holdings LLC · Privacy · 30 N Gould Street, Suite R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA

Our data protection contact is the same address. We respond to all privacy inquiries within thirty days.
