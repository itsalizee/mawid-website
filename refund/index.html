Refund policy.

Clear, plain language. The short version is on this page; if you have questions, talk to us.

Last updated · 3 June 2026

1. The short version

7-day free trial. Cancel anytime before it ends and you are never charged. After the trial, paid subscriptions are billed monthly and not refundable for partial months.

2. The free trial

Every new Mawid subscription includes a 7-day free trial. Your payment card is collected at signup but not charged. If you cancel before the end of day 7, you are not charged and your subscription ends.

3. Trial converts to paid

If you do not cancel before the trial ends, your card is charged the monthly price of the plan you selected (Starter, Professional, or Studio) and your subscription continues on a monthly billing cycle.

4. Monthly subscriptions

After the trial, your subscription is billed in advance on the same day of each month. We do not refund partial months. If you cancel mid-cycle, your subscription remains active through the end of the period you paid for, then ends. You are not billed again.

5. How to cancel

Sign in to your Mawid account, open Settings → Billing → Manage billing, and click Cancel subscription. The action is immediate and you receive an email confirmation. You can also email us at billing@mawid.ai and we will cancel for you.

6. Exceptions where we do refund

If we charged you in error (duplicate billing, billed after a confirmed cancellation, or billed despite a service outage that prevented you from using Mawid for a substantial portion of the period), email billing@mawid.ai with your account email and the date of the issue. We respond within 2 business days and process valid refunds within 5–10 business days, depending on your card issuer.

7. Chargebacks

Please email us before filing a chargeback. Disputes are quicker and easier to resolve directly. If a chargeback is filed without prior contact, your Mawid account may be suspended until the dispute is resolved.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our product evolves. Material changes are emailed to active subscribers at least 14 days before they take effect.

9. Contact

Billing questions: billing@mawid.ai. Anything else: our contact page.
