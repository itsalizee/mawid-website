02 FAQ

Pricing questions.

Beta participants are not billed during the active beta period (typically the first 60 days). Subscription billing begins after onboarding completes and you have processed your first ten paid bookings through Mawid.
The platform fee applies only to online payments processed through Mawid (deposits, full prepayment via Mada, KNET, Apple Pay, Tabby, or Tamara). It does not apply to in-person payments at your salon, cash, or any payment method outside the Mawid booking flow.
The customer's payment goes directly to your existing merchant account at Tap Payments, HyperPay, or Moyasar. Their processing fees (typically 2 to 3 percent) are charged separately by your processor. Mawid never holds your money.
