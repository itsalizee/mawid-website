06 FAQ

Common questions from Gulf operators.

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. The booking page is a regular web page that opens in their phone's browser. The confirmation lands in WhatsApp. They never sign up for anything.

Do you take a commission on each reservation?

No. Mawid is a flat monthly subscription — $180/month for restaurants. No per-reservation fees, no commissions, no markup on payments.

What about walk-ins?

Walk-ins still walk in. Mawid handles the reserved-in-advance side of your night. Your hostess marks walk-ins on the dashboard so you have one source of truth for who's in the building.

Does it handle table assignments and floor plans?

Mawid uses a capacity model — you tell us how many parties you can take at once (8 tables = 8 concurrent reservations) and we space them across the slot. Visual drag-and-drop floor plans are on the roadmap for the higher tier, not in V1.

Can guests reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Every confirmation includes a tap-to-reschedule link. The change reaches your dashboard in real time so the hostess always sees the truth.

Is the booking page in Arabic?

Yes, by default for guests with Arabic phones. English for everyone else. The toggle is one tap. RTL is native — not a translation glued on top of a Western product.

How long does it take to set up?

Under five minutes. You tell us your restaurant name, country, how many tables you can take at once, and your opening hours. We send you a booking link to paste in your Instagram bio. That's the install.

Which countries do you support?

Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, and Kuwait at launch. Each country gets its own defaults — prayer times, weekend days, Hijri awareness.
