02 The problem

Every casual restaurant in the Gulf runs reservations the same broken way.

Instagram DMs

Lost in the inbox.

Customer sends "Friday 8pm for four?" Your team replies six hours later. Customer already booked somewhere else.

WhatsApp threads

Fourteen conversations, no calendar.

Your host holds the whole night in their head. When they change shifts, tomorrow's reservations get half-forgotten.

Paper book

One book, twelve mistakes a night.

Crossed-out lines, party size in the wrong column, walk-ins jammed in the margins. No record of who's a regular.

Global platforms

Built for somewhere else.

Global reservation platforms are built and priced for big restaurant groups, and Arabic usually comes second.

03 How it works

Three steps. No app downloads. No setup fees.

Your guests' experience is intentionally simple — that's how reservations should be. Your team's experience is the same, just better.

01 · GUEST

Picks a time.

You share your booking link from your Instagram bio or WhatsApp status. Guest opens it, picks party size + time, enters their name and number. Thirty seconds.

02 · GUEST

Gets confirmed.

The confirmation screen shows the date in Hijri and Gregorian, adds it to their calendar in one tap, and has a WhatsApp button if plans change. Automated WhatsApp reminders are coming soon.

03 · YOU

See who's coming.

Your reservations dashboard shows tonight by hour: name, party size, phone, notes. Tap a reservation to mark them arrived. That's it.

04 What you get

Four things built for Gulf restaurants.

01

Arabic-first reservations.

Your booking page renders in Arabic or English based on the guest's phone. RTL layout is native, not retrofitted. Numerals format correctly. Times read naturally.

02

WhatsApp, one tap away.

Guests don't install anything or make an account. Your booking page and every confirmation have a WhatsApp button that opens a chat with you. Automated WhatsApp confirmations and reminders are coming soon; until then, you get an email for every new reservation.

03

Hijri + Gregorian, side by side.

Your booking page shows both calendars. Ramadan iftar and Eid reservations work like any other night, Friday mornings are closed by default for Saudi venues, and holiday closures go in in seconds. We built for the Gulf calendar, not against it.

04

Guest database that remembers.

The party of four from last spring? Mawid keeps their name, number, visit count and the food allergy you noted last time. Your team treats every regular like a regular — because the system does the remembering.

06 FAQ

Common questions from Gulf operators.

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. The booking page is a regular web page that opens in their phone's browser. They see their confirmation right away and never sign up for anything.

Do you take a commission on each reservation?

No. Mawid is a flat monthly subscription — $180/month on the Studio plan. No per-reservation fees, no commissions, no markup on payments.

What about walk-ins?

Walk-ins still walk in. Mawid handles the reserved-in-advance side of your night. The dashboard shows tonight's reservations by hour, so your host knows exactly what's booked.

Does it handle table assignments and floor plans?

Mawid uses a capacity model — you tell us how many parties you can take at once (8 tables = 8 concurrent reservations) and we space them across the slot. Visual drag-and-drop floor plans are on the roadmap for the higher tier, not in V1.

Can guests reschedule or cancel?

Yes. They tap the WhatsApp button on their confirmation to message you; your team cancels the old reservation and sends the booking link for a new time. One-tap self-serve changes are on the roadmap.

Is the booking page in Arabic?

Yes, automatically for guests whose phone is set to Arabic. English for everyone else. The toggle is one tap. RTL is native — not a translation glued on top of a Western product.

How long does it take to set up?

Under five minutes. You tell us your restaurant name, country, how many tables you can take at once, and your opening hours. We send you a booking link to paste in your Instagram bio. That's the install.

Which countries do you support?

Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, and Kuwait at launch. Each country gets its own defaults — prayer times, weekend days, Hijri awareness.
