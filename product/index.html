Take a deposit. Stop the no-show before it happens.

Mada, KNET, Apple Pay, Tabby, Tamara, and cards — all in the booking flow. Deposits captured at the moment of intent, not at the chair.

Native Mada and KNET

Through Tap, the merchant of record across the GCC. No Stripe-style "this card was declined" friction. Local cards work locally.

Refundable deposits

Configurable percentage per service. 20% for Color & Cut. 30% for Keratin. 40% for Bridal. Customer pays at booking, applied at checkout.

BNPL via Tabby & Tamara

For larger bookings (bridal, full-day packages), customers can split into 3 or 4 payments. You get paid upfront. They pay over time.

Settlement to your bank

Daily or weekly settlement to your local bank account. SAR, AED, QAR, KWD — paid in your native currency.

Where bookings actually happen — WhatsApp.

Confirmations, reminders, payment links, rescheduling, reactivation. Every customer touchpoint flows through WhatsApp, in their language. No accounts. No app downloads.

Verified WhatsApp Business sender

Your salon name appears as a verified sender, not a "+1" number. Customers see "Salon Rasha" in their inbox, not a stranger.

Per-country dialect AI

Saudi Arabic for KSA. Khaleeji for UAE/Qatar/Kuwait. Auto-detected from customer's number. Replies sound right, not translated.

Two-way conversational rebooking

Customer says "can I move to Saturday?" — Mawid offers the next 3 available slots inline. They tap one. Done. No friction, no app.

Smart reminder timing

24h reminder for low-risk customers. 24h + 2h for medium-risk. 48h, 24h, and 2h for high-risk (based on history). Tuned to per-customer no-show probability.

A model that predicts who won't show up.

Every booking gets a no-show risk score. Reactivation campaigns auto-trigger for lapsed VIPs. Customer LTV tracked per visit. Mawid's AI learns from your salon's history, not generic data.

No-show risk scoring

0-100% per booking. Driven by customer history, time-of-day patterns, deposit-paid status, weather, and 14 other signals. Updates in real time.

Auto-reactivation campaigns

Customer hasn't booked in 60 days? Auto-WhatsApp with a personalized offer. 90 days? Stylist-specific message. 120 days? VIP win-back. All hands-off.

LTV tracking per customer

Every visit, every service, every spend tracked. Lifetime value computed automatically. Top-100 VIPs flagged. New high-spenders surfaced.

Stylist productivity insights

Booking density, average ticket, repeat rate, idle time per stylist. Identify who's underbooked, who's running over, who's the rebooking magnet.

Run five locations like one.

Per-location calendars, staff, and inventory. Owner sees everything. Managers see their location. Staff see their bookings. Customer sees one brand.

Per-location independence

Each location has its own calendar, staff roster, hours, services, and pricing. Run Riyadh and Dubai locations on completely different schedules.

Three role tiers

Owner: everything across every location. Manager: their location, their team, their reports. Staff: their own bookings only. Permissions enforced at the database level.

Cross-location reports

Compare Riyadh vs Jeddah vs Dubai. Identify which location is bleeding bookings. Spot which stylist would do better in another branch.

Customer follows the brand

A customer can book at any location through one link. Their history, preferences, and notes follow them — even if they move cities.

Bilingual everywhere — English and Arabic.

Operators flip between EN and AR with one tap. Customers see the booking page in their browser language. WhatsApp messages match the customer's language preference. RTL is native, not retrofitted.

RTL as a first-class citizen

Every screen designed in both directions from day one. Calendar flows right-to-left. Reports right-align numbers correctly. No reversed icons.

Per-customer language pref

Customer A gets English WhatsApp messages. Customer B gets Arabic. Same salon, same booking flow — Mawid remembers each one's preference.

Western Arabic numerals
